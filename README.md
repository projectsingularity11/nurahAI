# Nurah AI

Nurah AI is a Muslim-focused daily companion for worship, learning, focus, and personal growth.

Core pillars:
- Prayer times and Salah Focus
- Qur’an and authentic hadith study
- Dhikr and duas
- AI-assisted planning and habits
- Self-improvement through weekly reflection

Nurah AI is a study and organization tool. It does not issue fatwas.
